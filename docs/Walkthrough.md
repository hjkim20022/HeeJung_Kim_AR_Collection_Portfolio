# Two-minute walkthrough practice

Use this script after you have built and checked the Power BI report. Adjust the wording to match what you personally completed and can demonstrate. The timed sections are a guide, not a claim that a video has been recorded.

## 0:00-0:20 - Explain the business question

"This is my accounts receivable and collections portfolio project. It uses fictional data for 40 customers. The question is: which customers have overdue balances, and where should a collections team focus its next review? The snapshot is September 30, 2026, and all amounts are Canadian dollars."

## 0:20-0:50 - Explain the overview

"The dataset has about $1.55 million outstanding. Of that, $980,236, or 63.3%, is overdue. Almost $472,000 is more than 90 days overdue. The aging chart shows where the outstanding balance sits. The monthly line shows cash collected; it is a different measure from the fixed-date receivables balance."

## 0:50-1:20 - Connect the finding to an action

"The five largest overdue customer balances account for about $282,806. I would start by reviewing those accounts with their owners. There is also $263,311 in disputed overdue invoices across the portfolio. I would route those invoices for dispute resolution before routine reminders. These are proposed actions; I am not claiming that this project recovered money."

## 1:20-1:50 - Demonstrate validation

"To avoid double-counting, I grouped payment allocations by invoice before joining them to invoices. The invoice total is $4,873,723.46, and payments are $3,324,752.27. Their difference matches the $1,548,971.19 outstanding balance. I also checked partial payments, date cutoffs and aging boundaries, then compared the report totals with the reference results."

## 1:50-2:00 - Close with the manager's next step

"The worklist turns the analysis into an invoice-level review, with the account owner, outstanding amount, age and dispute reason. It shows what to investigate next and why."

## Recording sequence

Start on Overview. Move to Customers for the top-five finding. Drill through from customer C027 to its worklist, then show one validation example. Read naturally and pause between ideas. Display actual report pages, not the design-preview PDF.
